How Multilingual are multilingual LLMs?

  • 65 out of the 95 languages supported by ChatGPT are Indo-European languages
  • Most texts in Red Pajama 2 are English: 14.5 billion out of 20.8 billion. All are Indo-European languages
  • Over 40% documents of Common Crawl are English

Empirical Strategy

  • Data: Parliamentary texts from UK, Basque, HK, Taiwan
  • Task: "What is the policy area of the following text according to Comparative Agendas Project?"
  • Synthetic data pairs to test the effect of linguistic and contextual similarity

Takeaways

  • Linguistic and contextual biases exist
  • Most of the Gen LLMs are no better than BERT
  • Regional LLMs are not necessarily better for that region
  • Reducing parameters seem to make things worse
